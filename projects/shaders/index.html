Shader experiments

Small experiments in maths, motion and colour, originally made in Shadertoy. Each one runs live on your GPU. To keep things smooth, only one plays at a time: scroll to a preview or press its Play button, and open View code to read the GLSL.

Mandelbrot zoom

A live Mandelbrot fractal.

A continuous dive into the Mandelbrot set. The purple bands mark how quickly each point escapes; the zoom starts again every nine seconds. Here it's capped at 3,004 steps per pixel (the code allows 100,000) so it runs smoothly.
